EU Contestation

Interactive estimates of how intensely parties contested European integration. Select parties below to compare contestation intensity across elections.

Contestation intensity (theta) runs from 0 to 1. Higher values mean a party combined Eurosceptic positioning with sustained attention to European issues in that election. Estimates come from a Bayesian measurement model combining coded manifesto and media texts with expert surveys, covering 25 selected parties in 28 European countries from 1945 to 2022.

This page presents data from Seimel, Tracking a Sleeping Giant: Political Contestation of European Integration, 1945 to 2022, forthcoming at the European Journal of Political Research.

Each dot is the posterior mean for one party in one election, with 95 percent credible intervals. Short ticks at zero mark elections with no measurable contestation. Estimates are distinct per election, so points are not connected by lines.
