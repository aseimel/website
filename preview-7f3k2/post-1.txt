A citable party-position panel is now available for research.
It covers 708 parties in 65 countries from 1944 to 2025.

The panel contains 4,924 election-year rows, with one row per party segment at an election. For each estimate, it records economic left-right and cultural cosmopolitan-traditionalist positions on bounded 0 to 1 scales, posterior uncertainty intervals, and metadata showing which evidence informed the estimate.

The estimates come from a Bayesian dynamic item-response model that combines manifesto and media text codings with expert survey placements. The image is one panel from the paper: nine party-position landmarks on the two dimensions, with the uncertainty interval drawn through each point.
