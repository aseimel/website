Party2D

Interactive party-position estimates. Select parties below to compare economic and cultural positions over time.

Party2D tracks where parties stand on two simple scales, economic left versus right and cultural cosmopolitan versus traditionalist. It covers 708 parties in 65 countries from 1944 to 2025. These are statistical estimates based on several expert surveys of party positions combined with coded manifesto and media texts, placed on one comparable scale with uncertainty shown for every estimate.
